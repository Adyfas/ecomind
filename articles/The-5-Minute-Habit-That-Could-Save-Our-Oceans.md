### The 5-Minute Habit That Could Save Our Oceans

You’ve seen the videos.  
The turtle with a straw in its nose.  
The seabird with a stomach full of plastic.  
The beach covered in bottles and bags.

It’s heartbreaking.  
And it feels impossible to fix.

But what if the solution wasn’t about grand gestures —  
but about **one tiny habit** you can start today…  
in just **five minutes**?

---

### The Habit: **Carry a Reusable Bag — Always**

Not for shopping.  
Not for “being eco-friendly.”  
But for **saving lives**.

Every time you leave home — even if you’re just going to the corner store, the gym, or your friend’s place —  
**take a foldable reusable bag with you.**

It takes 5 seconds to grab it.  
It fits in your pocket, your shoe, your purse.  
And it can prevent **one plastic bag** from ending up in the ocean.

> 🌊 **8 million tons** of plastic enter our oceans every year.  
> **1 plastic bag** can kill a sea turtle.  
> **1 reusable bag** = 500+ plastic bags saved over its lifetime.

You don’t need to give up coffee, plastic wrap, or takeout.  
Just carry a bag.

That’s it.

---

### Why This One Habit Changes Everything

#### ✅ It’s Simple
No training. No app. No guilt.  
Just a bag. Always.

#### ✅ It’s Contagious
When you pull out a reusable bag at the market,  
someone notices.  
They ask, “Where’d you get that?”  
And suddenly — you’ve started a conversation.

#### ✅ It’s Powerful
One bag saved = one less piece of plastic in the water.  
One less chance for a fish to mistake it for food.  
One less chance for microplastics to end up in your dinner.

> 💡 *If 100 people in your neighborhood did this every day, they’d save 18,250 plastic bags per year.*

---

### What If You Forget?

That’s okay.  
We all do.

But here’s the trick:  
Put your bag **where you’ll see it** —  
- On your keys  
- In your backpack  
- Tucked inside your shoe  
- Hung on your doorknob

And when you forget?  
**Say no to the bag.**  
Just carry your items out.  
You’ll be surprised how often you can.

> “I don’t need a bag for three apples.”  
> “I can hold this bottle in my hand.”  
> “I’ll reuse the one I already have.”

That’s not inconvenience.  
That’s awareness.

---

### The Bigger Picture: Small Actions, Big Waves

You don’t have to be perfect.  
You don’t have to compost, go plastic-free, or quit meat.

You just have to **remember your bag**.

Because oceans don’t need heroes.  
They need **millions of people doing small things, consistently**.

And every time you carry a bag —  
you’re not just carrying groceries.  
You’re carrying **hope**.

---

### Final Thought: You Are Already Part of the Solution

You’re reading this.  
That means you care.

You don’t need to change your life.  
You just need to **change your habit**.

Start today:  
👉 Grab a bag.  
👉 Put it where you’ll see it.  
👉 Take it with you tomorrow.

And when you do —  
you’ve already done more than 99% of people.

That’s not enough?  
It’s more than enough.

Because change doesn’t start with a movement.  
It starts with a **single bag**.

And you?  
You just picked yours up.
