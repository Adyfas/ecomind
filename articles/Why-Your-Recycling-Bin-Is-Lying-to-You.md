### Why Your Recycling Bin Is Lying to You

You rinse the yogurt cup.  
You flatten the cardboard.  
You toss the plastic bottle into the blue bin — proud of yourself.

But here’s the truth no one tells you:  
**Only 1 in 10 plastic bottles you recycle actually becomes a new bottle.**

The rest?  
They’re dumped.  
Burned.  
Or shipped overseas — often ending up in rivers and oceans.

Why?  
Because **recycling is not a magic solution**.  
It’s a **marketing tool** — invented by plastic companies to make you feel better about buying more plastic.

You’re not failing.  
The system is.

---

### The Great Recycling Illusion

Every plastic container has a number inside the recycling symbol.  
That number?  
It’s not a “green badge.” It’s a **cost code**.

| Number   | Type            | Recyclable?               |
| -------- | --------------- | ------------------------- |
| #1 PET   | Water bottles   | ✅ Usually yes            |
| #2 HDPE  | Milk jugs       | ✅ Usually yes            |
| #3 PVC   | Shower curtains | ❌ Rarely                 |
| #4 LDPE  | Plastic bags    | ❌ Never in curbside bins |
| #5 PP    | Yogurt tubs     | ⚠️ Sometimes              |
| #6 PS    | Styrofoam       | ❌ Almost never           |
| #7 Other | Mixed plastics  | ❌ Almost never           |

> 🚨 **Plastic bags, wrappers, and film** are the #1 cause of recycling plant shutdowns.  
> They tangle machines. Cost millions. And often get sent to landfills anyway.

Even “clean” items get rejected if they’re contaminated —  
a single greasy pizza box can ruin a whole truckload of paper.

### What You _Should_ Be Doing Instead

#### 1. 🚫 **Stop Relying on Recycling**

Recycling is the _last_ resort — not the first.  
The real hierarchy?  
**Refuse > Reduce > Reuse > Recycle > Rot (compost) > Landfill**

Ask yourself before buying:

> _“Can I avoid this packaging entirely?”_

#### 2. 🛒 **Choose Package-Free or Refillable**

- Buy rice, beans, and soap in bulk with your own jars.
- Use shampoo bars instead of plastic bottles.
- Bring your own cup to coffee shops (many give discounts!).

#### 3. 📦 **Recycle Right — Or Don’t Recycle at All**

If you _do_ recycle:

- Rinse containers. No food residue.
- Keep lids on plastic bottles (they’re recyclable now!).
- **Never** put plastic bags, Styrofoam, or dirty pizza boxes in the bin.
- When in doubt? **Leave it out.** Contamination hurts more than omission.

> 💡 _Pro tip: Google “What can I recycle in [Your City]” — rules vary wildly._

#### 4. 🗑️ **Know What’s Truly Non-Recyclable**

These belong in the trash — not the blue bin:

- Toothpaste tubes
- Coffee pods (unless branded for recycling)
- Dirty paper napkins
- Broken glass (unless your city accepts it)
- Mix-material packaging (chip bags, juice boxes)

### The Real Hero? You — Not the Bin

The planet doesn’t need more recycling bins.  
It needs **more people who refuse to accept waste as normal**.

When you:

- Say “no” to a plastic straw,
- Bring your own bag to the store,
- Choose glass over plastic,  
  — you’re not just reducing trash.  
  You’re sending a message:
  > _“I won’t be complicit in this system.”_

And that’s how change begins.

---

### Final Thought: You’re Not the Problem — But You’re the Solution

We’ve been told:  
_“Recycle to save the planet.”_

But the truth is:  
_“Stop buying so much plastic, and you won’t need to recycle.”_

You don’t need to be perfect.  
You just need to be **aware**.

Start today:  
👉 Pick one item you always throw away — and find a reusable alternative.  
👉 Share this with someone who still thinks recycling is enough.  
👉 Feel proud — because you’re waking up.

And that?  
That’s the most powerful act of sustainability there is.
